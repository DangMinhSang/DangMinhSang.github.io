PHẦN III: SPEAKING – SPEAKING TEST 2

Speaking Test 2

Tổng thời gian thi: ~12 phút | Gồm 3 phần: Tương tác xã hội, Thảo luận giải pháp, Phát triển chủ đề

PART 1

Social Interaction (3 phút)

Giám khảo sẽ hỏi bạn một số câu hỏi về các chủ đề quen thuộc (Mẹo: Nên trả lời từ 2-3 câu trọn vẹn, có giải thích lý do).

Topic 1: Holiday

Câu 1: What did you do on your last holiday?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"On my last holiday, I went to Sam Son beach with my family. We spent three days swimming, enjoying fresh seafood, and relaxing after a busy working period."

Câu 2: Who do you prefer spending your holiday with?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"I prefer spending my holidays with my family because I love them dearly, and it is a wonderful chance for all family members to bond and create memorable moments together."

Câu 3: Do you prefer going on holiday abroad? Why / Why not?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"At the moment, I prefer traveling domestically because going abroad requires a large budget and extensive planning, whereas domestic trips are more affordable and accessible."

Topic 2: TV Programs

Câu 1: How many hours a day do you watch television?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"I usually spend about one hour a day watching television, mostly in the evening to catch up on the daily news and unwind."

Câu 2: Which TV programs do you like best?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"I particularly enjoy watching VTV news and scientific documentaries because these channels provide me with valuable knowledge about current affairs and technology."

Câu 3: Do you prefer watching TV alone or with other people? Why?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"I prefer watching TV with my family because we can share our thoughts, comment on entertaining shows, and discuss interesting news together."

PART 2

Solution Discussion (4 phút)

Tình huống (Situation):
If you won a lottery of 1 billion VND, what would you do with the money?
Buy a new house
Start a business
Deposit the money in the bank
Lựa chọn đề xuất: Buy a new house

"If I were lucky enough to win a lottery of 1 billion VND, I would definitely choose to buy a house. Firstly, having a stable home is essential for an independent and secure life. Currently, I am living with my parents, so owning an apartment will give me my own living space. While starting a business is appealing, it carries a substantial risk of financial loss if one lacks managerial experience. Depositing money in the bank offers safety, but inflation can diminish its real value over time. Therefore, investing in real estate by buying a house is the most practical and reliable choice."

PART 3

Topic Development (5 phút)

Chủ đề thảo luận:

"Online courses have many advantages for learners."

Provide new learning experience
Access interactive multimedia, global lectures, and diverse learning tools.
Be more convenient to study
Study anywhere and anytime without traveling; flexible schedule fitting personal rhythm.
Be independent in learning
Develop self-discipline, autonomy, and time-management skills.
Your own ideas
Cost efficiency: save on transport, accommodation, and physical textbook fees.
Bài phát triển chủ đề mẫu:

"It is undeniable that online courses offer tremendous benefits to contemporary learners. First and foremost, online courses provide a brand new learning experience. Students can access high-quality lectures from prestigious universities across the globe, supported by rich digital resources. Secondly, convenience is a decisive factor; learners can study at their own pace from the comfort of their homes, saving valuable commute time. Furthermore, self-paced online education fosters independence, training students to become autonomous and responsible for their own learning curve. Lastly, online programs are generally much more affordable than traditional schooling."

Câu hỏi mở rộng (Further Questions):

1. What advice would you give someone on how to study English effectively?

Gợi ý trả lời: "I would advise them to immerse themselves in English daily by listening to podcasts, watching movies with English subtitles, and practicing speaking without fearing mistakes."

2. Do you think being a student is the best time of your life?

Gợi ý trả lời: "Yes, I do. Student life is a unique period when you have boundless energy, great friendships, and freedom to learn without heavy financial burdens."

PHẦN III: SPEAKING – SPEAKING TEST 3

Speaking Test 3

Tổng thời gian thi: ~12 phút | Gồm 3 phần: Tương tác xã hội, Thảo luận giải pháp, Phát triển chủ đề

PART 1

Social Interaction (3 phút)

Giám khảo sẽ hỏi bạn về các chủ đề quen thuộc (Music, English).

Topic 1: Music

Câu 1: Do you like music? If yes, what kind of music do you like best? Why?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"Yes, I am a huge fan of music. I particularly enjoy acoustic pop and classical music because the soothing melodies help me relieve stress after intense study sessions."

Câu 2: What is the most popular kind of music in your country?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"In Vietnam, V-pop and ballad songs are immensely popular, especially among young people, as they feature catchy melodies and relatable lyrics."

Câu 3: How is music good or not good for people?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"Music is predominantly beneficial because it stimulates mental creativity, lifts your mood, and connects people emotionally."

Topic 2: English

Câu 1: Did you learn English at secondary school?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"Yes, I began learning English when I was in secondary school. Although the curriculum focused mainly on grammar at that time, it built my initial foundation."

Câu 2: What do you think about how English is taught at school now?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"Nowadays, English teaching has improved with more communicative activities, multimedia aids, and opportunities to practice speaking compared to the past."

Câu 3: In what ways has learning English improved your life?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"Learning English has opened up access to international scientific literature, allowed me to communicate with foreign peers, and broadened my career prospects."

PART 2

Solution Discussion (4 phút)

Tình huống (Situation):
An optional subject will be added to the curriculum for secondary students in your city. Which do you think is the best option for students?
Photography
Swimming
Dancing
Lựa chọn đề xuất: Swimming

"Among the three options, I strongly believe that swimming is the best choice to incorporate into the secondary curriculum. First of all, swimming is not merely a sport but an indispensable survival skill that protects students against drowning accidents. Moreover, regular swimming provides a full-body workout, enhancing cardiovascular endurance and physical growth during adolescence. In contrast, photography requires expensive camera gear that many families cannot afford, and dancing may not appeal to all students equally. Therefore, swimming delivers the greatest health and safety advantages."

PART 3

Topic Development (5 phút)

Chủ đề thảo luận:

"It is good to learn about a culture when you learn a language."

Language: best means of culture
Language reflects cultural history, social values, and idioms.
Better communication
Understanding cultural etiquette ensures polite, respectful conversations.
Avoid culture shocks
Minimizes misunderstandings and embarrassing faux pas in international environments.
Your own ideas
Enhances emotional empathy and appreciation of world diversity.
Bài phát triển chủ đề mẫu:

"Learning a language should invariably be accompanied by studying its cultural context. First, language is the primary vehicle of culture; idioms and vocabulary are deeply rooted in historical traditions. Second, cultural awareness enables more natural and polite communication, preventing awkward misunderstandings. Third, understanding local customs shields travelers and students from severe culture shock when interacting with native speakers. In short, cultural competence transforms linguistic knowledge into practical fluency."

Câu hỏi mở rộng (Further Questions):

1. Do you think it is good to understand other people's cultures?

Gợi ý trả lời: "Definitely. It promotes mutual respect, tolerance, and eliminates prejudice in our increasingly globalized community."

2. What would you introduce first to a foreigner about Vietnamese culture?

Gợi ý trả lời: "I would proudly introduce Vietnamese culinary traditions, especially Pho and regional street food, alongside our hospitable family values."

PART 1

Social Interaction (3 phút)

Giám khảo sẽ hỏi bạn về Favorite Places & School Subjects.

Topic 1: Favorite Places

Câu 1: Where do you usually go when you have spare time?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"In my spare time, I often visit a quiet café or the municipal library where I can read books in a tranquil atmosphere."

Câu 2: Are there many interesting places where you live for people to go at weekends?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"Yes, my city features numerous lively night markets, public green parks, and scenic lakeside promenades that are packed on weekends."

Câu 3: In what way is a café a great place for people to enjoy their free time?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"A café provides a pleasant ambiance with background music, allowing people to either concentrate on personal tasks or socialize with friends."

Topic 2: Subjects at School

Câu 1: What subject did you like most when you were at school?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"Mathematics and Computer Science were my absolute favorites because solving logical problems gave me an immense sense of accomplishment."

Câu 2: Do you think English is a good subject for all students to learn at school? Why?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"Yes, absolutely. English is a global lingua franca, so learning it equips students with an essential tool for higher education and global careers."

Câu 3: Do you think students are learning too many subjects at school now?
Câu trả lời mẫu B2:

"In my opinion, yes. Students often face an overloaded academic curriculum, which leaves them with little time to cultivate soft skills or pursue personal passions."

PART 2

Solution Discussion (4 phút)

Tình huống (Situation):
You are going to take your secondary school students to the cinema. What kind of film do you think is best for the students?
Cartoon
Action movies
Detective movies
Lựa chọn đề xuất: Detective movies

"Taking secondary students to watch a detective movie would be the most suitable option. At secondary age, teenagers are naturally curious and eager to engage in deductive thinking. A detective movie presents engaging puzzles and plot twists that encourage critical reasoning and group discussion. On the other hand, cartoons might feel too childish for this age group, while action films often contain excessive violence that is inappropriate for young audiences. Thus, a detective film strikes the ideal balance between intellectual stimulation and wholesome entertainment."

PART 3

Topic Development (5 phút)

Chủ đề thảo luận:

"Punishment by teachers at school is good for students."

Obey teachers
Instills respect for classroom rules and authority.
Minimise misbehaviour
Deters disruptive acts and maintains a fair learning environment.
Concentrate better on study
Encourages students to take responsibilities for their academic tasks.
Your own ideas
Punishment must be constructive (e.g., extra tasks, reflections) rather than harsh or humiliating.
Bài phát triển chủ đề mẫu:

"Reasonable and constructive disciplinary measures play an important role in student development. First, fair disciplinary rules maintain order in the classroom, ensuring that disruptive behavior does not hinder others from studying. Second, it teaches teenagers accountability and consequences for their actions. However, it is vital that punishments are pedagogical—such as writing reflective essays or community tasks—rather than psychological humiliation or corporal punishment, which could foster resentment."

PART 2

Solution Discussion (4 phút)

Tình huống (Situation):
Your town plans to organize an Open Festival and many visitors will come. Which job below do you think would be most suitable for you?
A tour guide
A traffic instructor
A master of ceremony (MC)
Lựa chọn đề xuất: A tour guide

"If my town hosts an Open Festival, being a tour guide would be the most suitable and enjoyable role for me. As a local resident, I am deeply familiar with our historical monuments, culinary spots, and cultural stories, which enables me to present our hometown enthusiastically to foreign and domestic guests. Working as a tour guide also allows me to practice interpersonal communication and foreign language skills. In contrast, directing traffic is physically exhausting, and hosting as an MC demands professional stage skills that I have not been trained for."

PART 3

Topic Development (5 phút)

Chủ đề thảo luận:

"Smoking should be banned in public places."

Smoker's health
Helps smokers cut down consumption and reduces cardiovascular/lung risks.
Second-hand smoker's health
Protects innocent children, pregnant women, and workers from toxic passive smoke.
Financial burden
Reduces public healthcare expenditures on tobacco-related diseases.
Your own ideas
Creates cleaner public environments with less cigarette litter.
Bài phát triển chủ đề mẫu:

"Enforcing a comprehensive ban on smoking in public venues is an urgent necessity. Above all, second-hand smoke poses severe health hazards to non-smokers, especially children and elderly citizens who have the right to breathe clean air. Furthermore, restricting smoking zones encourages active smokers to reduce their habit, leading to fewer chronic illnesses and alleviating the heavy financial strain on national healthcare budgets. Public spaces will also be significantly cleaner without discarded cigarette butts."
